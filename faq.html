Frequently asked questions

Who can join the association?

Every student of the Faculty of Mathematical and Computer Science. Create an account to become a member.

Does membership cost anything?

No. Membership and all resources are free.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use the Forgot your password link on the log in page. We email you a link that works for one hour.

Where do I find past exams?

Open Resources, choose your program and level, then your course. Past exams are listed under each course.

How can I share my notes?

Send them through the contact page, choosing the subject Resource problem or request. The resources team checks each file before publishing it.

How do I become an admin?

Admin access is given by an existing admin to elected committee members. You cannot request it through the site.
