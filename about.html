About the association

The student association of the Faculty of Mathematical and Computer Science (FMCS) at the University of Gezira, run by students for students.

Our purpose

We make studying here easier and more connected. We organize learning events, support student projects, and keep course material where every student can find it.

What we do

  • Run workshops, talks, and problem-solving sessions throughout the year.
  • Publish lectures, notes, references, and past exams for every program and level.
  • Support student teams building open-source projects.
  • Welcome new students and connect them with senior students.
  • Speak for students when the faculty asks for feedback.

How we work

Open to everyone
Every student in the faculty can join, whatever their program or level.
Shared knowledge
What one student learns should help the next one, so we share notes and code openly.
Run by elected students
The committee is elected by members and reports on its work each term.
