Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026.

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your full name, email address, phone number, student ID number, program, and academic level. If you use the contact form we also keep your message.

Why we collect it

To run your account, show you the right resources, contact you about events you asked about, and answer your messages. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How we protect it

Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, so nobody can read them. Only elected committee members with admin accounts can see member details.

Your choices

You can edit your details in Settings at any time. To delete your account and data, send a request through the contact page.

Cookies

We use one session cookie to keep you logged in and one preference cookie for your theme. We use no tracking or advertising cookies.
